# SOAI Algiers Overview

## Organization

School of AI Algiers (SOAI) is organized around a structured internal system designed to support its educational, technical, creative, operational, and community activities.

The organization follows a hierarchical structure. At the top of the hierarchy are the Lead, Co-Lead, and HR Managers. The remaining departments are organized into three main categories:

- Creative
- IT
- Operations

## Internal Structure

SOAI's internal structure is composed of different departments and management structures, each with specific responsibilities.

The main structures described include:

- Technical Department
- Production Department
- Development Department
- Marketing & External Relations
- Design Department
- Logistics
- Human Resources & Projects

## Technical Department

The Technical Department is described as the club's "knowledge beacon."

Membership in the Technical Department is limited to contributors who have demonstrated technical competence, commitment, and willingness to actively participate throughout the season.

The department is not open to new members joining the club. However, its learning opportunities, including workshops and educational activities, remain accessible to all SOAI members.

The Technical Department focuses on:

- AI education
- Applied AI
- AI research
- Knowledge archiving
- AI safety

## Production Department

The Production Department brings together the Content Creation and Multimedia divisions under one structure.

This organization aims to reduce overlap between the two divisions, clarify task allocation, strengthen collaboration, and create a unified creative direction.

The Production Department is responsible for:

- Shaping SOAI's public image
- Ensuring content reflects SOAI's values, identity, and vision
- Coordinating Content Creation and Multimedia
- Working with Marketing and External Relations
- Promoting events and collaborations
- Maintaining a consistent presence through diverse content
- Managing SOAI's social media accounts and communication through them

### Multimedia

The Multimedia division works across different forms of media to communicate SOAI's story.

Its responsibilities include:

- Photography
- Videography
- Reels
- Motion graphics
- Potential podcast production
- Visual content design and editing
- Documenting events, projects, achievements, and community milestones
- Proposing new multimedia formats and approaches

### Content Creation

The Content Creation division is responsible for written communication and content planning.

Its responsibilities include:

- Preparing communication strategies and content plans for events and projects
- Writing scripts, captions, announcements, articles, and other written material
- Maintaining quality and consistency in communication
- Staying informed about SOAI activities
- Working with Multimedia to transform ideas into visual content
- Working with the Technical Department on educational initiatives and AI-related content
- Contributing to SOAI's tone of voice and communication strategy

## Development Department

The Development Department is responsible for software engineering-related activities.

A major focus of the department is creating the SOAI Learning Platform, a project intended to provide members with opportunities to learn, collaborate, and contribute meaningfully.

The department's ambitions include:

- Teaching software engineering fundamentals to members
- Providing software engineering workshops open to all members
- Exploring the integration of AI into software engineering
- Laying the foundation for the SOAI Learning Platform
- Potentially completing the SOAI Learning Platform

## Marketing & External Relations

The Marketing & External Relations department focuses on representing SOAI and managing relationships with external organizations and opportunities.

Its responsibilities include:

- Scouting and participating in opportunities to represent SOAI
- Participating in conferences, events, stands, and similar activities
- Preparing a sponsorship plan
- Focusing sponsorship efforts on major events
- Building and updating the External Relations database
- Representing SOAI in front of sponsors
- Managing event and club budgets
- Ensuring proper allocation of resources

## Design Department

The Design Department focuses on visual design and creative representation.

Its responsibilities include:

- Graphic design
- Printable materials
- Visual identity
- Posters
- Stickers
- Social media posts
- Certificates
- UI/UX design
- Teaching graphic design and UI/UX
- Supporting members in their creative development
- Working on the UI/UX of projects developed by the Development Department

## Logistics

The Logistics team is responsible for ensuring the smooth operation of events on their day of execution.

Its responsibilities include:

- Preparing detailed logistical plans
- Identifying event requirements
- Assigning responsibilities
- Anticipating potential challenges
- Coordinating event schedules
- Organizing D-Day operations
- Managing logistical resources and inventory
- Ensuring required materials are available on time
- Conducting pre-event briefings
- Conducting simulations and coordination meetings
- Ensuring members understand their responsibilities

## Human Resources & Projects

Human Resources and Projects are described as management structures rather than official departments open to members during reintegration.

They consist of the managers of their respective structures.

Members are encouraged to stay informed about project management opportunities and can apply for Project Manager positions for upcoming projects.
