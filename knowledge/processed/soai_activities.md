# SOAI Algiers Activities

## Welcome Day

Welcome Day is a special event dedicated to welcoming new members of the School of AI Algiers. It introduces the organization's vision and values and gives new members an overview of the learning journey and community experience ahead.

## General Assembly

The General Assembly is an important institutional event dedicated to presenting the objectives and action plan and formalizing the club's internal organization.

## AI Camp — Integration Program

AI Camp is an internal event dedicated to artificial intelligence learning for School of AI Algiers members.

The program provides learning paths adapted to members' different levels and focuses on progressive integration through practical workshops and learning activities.

It has two phases : the online phase which is focused on learning machine learning fundamentals like supervised learning, mathematics for AI. The second phase is a specialization where our participants can choose a track like deep learning, computer vision, reinforcement learning, agentic ai and natural language processing.

## haick — National Datathon

haick is a national datathon organized by the School of AI Algiers. It has been organized since 2021 and is presented as the first datathon in Algeria.

The event includes:

- Pre-event workshops
- Conferences
- The use of real-world datasets

The event provides an opportunity for sharing, competition, and connection between students interested in artificial intelligence across Algeria.

## AI Real Camp — Internal Mini-Datathon

AI Real Camp is an beginner-oriented, internal mini-datathon for School of AI Algiers members.

Its goal is to allow members to practice artificial intelligence in a practical and collaborative environment, strengthen their skills, and encourage teamwork.

## AI Summit

AI Summit is an event that brings together researchers, students, and industry professionals through conferences and panels.

The event focuses on the connection between academic research and industrial applications of artificial intelligence, with the goal of inspiring and guiding future Algerian engineers.

# SOAI Algiers Activities

It introduces the organization's vision and values and gives new members an overview of the learning journey and community experience ahead.

## General Assembly

## AI Camp — Integration Program

AI Camp is an internal event dedicated to artificial intelligence learning for School of AI Algiers members.

The program provides learning paths adapted to members' different levels and focuses on progressive integration through practical workshops and learning activities.

The Technical Department also contributes to the educational mission of SOAI through workshops and educational activities that are accessible to the wider SOAI community.

## hAIck — National Datathon

hAIck is a national datathon organized by the School of AI Algiers.

It has been organized since 2021 and is presented as the first datathon in Algeria.

The event includes:

- Pre-event workshops
- Conferences
- Real-world datasets
- Practical challenges

hAIck provides an opportunity for sharing, competition, and connection between students interested in artificial intelligence across Algeria.

## AI Real Camp — Internal Mini-Datathon

AI Real Camp is an internal mini-datathon for School of AI Algiers members.

Its goal is to allow members to practice artificial intelligence in a practical and collaborative environment, strengthen their skills, and encourage teamwork.

## AI Summit

AI Summit is an event that brings together researchers, students, and industry professionals through conferences and panels.

The event focuses on the connection between academic research and industrial applications of artificial intelligence.

Its goal is to inspire and guide future Algerian engineers.

## Educational Activities

Education is an important part of SOAI's activities.

The organization provides learning opportunities through workshops, educational content, and practical activities.

The Technical Department contributes to this mission through:

- AI education
- Educational content
- Workshops
- Learning curricula
- Practical AI projects
- AI research activities
- Knowledge sharing

These educational opportunities can be made accessible to SOAI members beyond the Technical Department itself.

## Software Engineering Education

SOAI also supports software engineering education through the Development Department.

Activities include teaching software engineering fundamentals through workshops that are open to members.

The Development Department also explores how artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly integrated into software engineering.

## Knowledge Sharing

SOAI activities also contribute to preserving and sharing technical knowledge.

Knowledge archiving includes documenting technical resources, projects, and educational material so that knowledge can be preserved and passed on to future generations.

## AI Safety and Responsible AI

AI safety is part of SOAI's technical activities.

The organization promotes responsible and ethical AI development by raising awareness of:

- AI safety
- AI alignment
- Ethical considerations in technical work
