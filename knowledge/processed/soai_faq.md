# SOAI Algiers Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the School of AI Algiers?

The School of AI Algiers (SOAI) is an organization focused on artificial intelligence education, practical projects, research, knowledge sharing, and community activities.

## How is SOAI organized?

SOAI follows a hierarchical internal structure.

At the top are the Lead, Co-Lead, and HR Managers. The remaining departments are organized into three main categories:

- Creative
- IT
- Operations

## What departments exist in SOAI?

The departments and structures described by SOAI include:

- Technical Department
- Production Department
- Development Department
- Marketing & External Relations
- Design Department
- Logistics
- Human Resources & Projects

## What does the Technical Department do?

The Technical Department is described as SOAI's "knowledge beacon."

It focuses on:

- AI education
- Applied AI
- AI research
- Knowledge archiving
- AI safety

## Can all SOAI members participate in Technical Department activities?

The Technical Department itself is limited to contributors who demonstrate technical competence, commitment, and willingness to participate actively.

However, its learning opportunities, including workshops and educational activities, remain accessible to all SOAI members.

## What is the Production Department?

The Production Department is an umbrella structure that brings together the Content Creation and Multimedia divisions.

It focuses on representing SOAI through meaningful, consistent, and high-quality content.

## What does the Multimedia division do?

The Multimedia division works with different media to communicate SOAI's story.

Its activities include:

- Photography
- Videography
- Reels
- Motion graphics
- Potential podcast production
- Event and project documentation
- Visual content production

## What does the Content Creation division do?

The Content Creation division handles written communication and content planning.

It produces:

- Scripts
- Captions
- Announcements
- Articles
- Other written communication

It also works with Multimedia and the Technical Department on communication and educational content.

## What does the Development Department do?

The Development Department leads software engineering-related activities.

Its goals include teaching software engineering fundamentals, exploring the integration of AI into software engineering, and developing the SOAI Learning Platform.

## What is the SOAI Learning Platform?

The SOAI Learning Platform is a planned software engineering project intended to provide members with opportunities to learn, collaborate, and make meaningful contributions.

The Development Department is responsible for laying its foundation and potentially completing the project.

## What does Marketing & External Relations do?

Marketing & External Relations is responsible for representing SOAI and managing external relationships.

Its responsibilities include:

- Finding opportunities to represent SOAI
- Participating in conferences and events
- Preparing sponsorship plans
- Managing the External Relations database
- Representing SOAI to sponsors
- Managing event and club budgets

## What does the Design Department do?

The Design Department is responsible for visual and creative work.

It works on:

- Graphic design
- Printable materials
- Posters
- Stickers
- Social media posts
- Certificates
- UI/UX

It also teaches design and contributes to the UI/UX of projects developed by the Development Department.

## What does the Logistics team do?

The Logistics team ensures that events run smoothly on their day of execution.

Its responsibilities include:

- Event planning
- Scheduling
- Resource management
- Inventory management
- Responsibility assignment
- Pre-event briefings
- Simulations
- D-Day coordination

## What is Welcome Day?

Welcome Day is an event dedicated to welcoming new SOAI members.

It introduces the organization's vision and values and gives new members an overview of the learning and community experience.

## What is the General Assembly?

The General Assembly is an institutional event where objectives and the action plan are presented and the club's internal organization is formalized.

## What is AI Camp?

AI Camp is an internal artificial intelligence learning event for SOAI members.

It provides learning paths adapted to different levels and uses practical workshops and learning activities.

## What is hAIck?

hAIck is a national datathon organized by the School of AI Algiers.

It includes workshops, conferences, real-world datasets, and opportunities for competition, sharing, and connection between AI students across Algeria.

## What is AI Real Camp?

AI Real Camp is an internal mini-datathon for SOAI members.

It provides a practical and collaborative environment where members can apply artificial intelligence, strengthen their skills, and develop teamwork.

## What is AI Summit?

AI Summit is an event that brings together researchers, students, and industry professionals through conferences and panels.

It focuses on connecting academic AI research with industrial applications.

## What kind of projects does SOAI work on?

SOAI works on technical and research-oriented projects involving areas such as:

- Artificial intelligence
- Robotics
- Reinforcement learning
- Scientific machine learning
- Physics-informed learning
- Multi-agent systems
- UAVs
- AI safety

## What is Language-Guided Robotic Manipulation?

Language-Guided Robotic Manipulation is a multimodal deep reinforcement learning project that enables a simulated robotic arm to understand natural-language instructions and interact with objects.

It combines language embeddings, visual perception, spatial information, and PPO-based visuomotor control.

## What is Physics-Aware Transformer?

Physics-Aware Transformer is a research framework combining Fourier Neural Operators with Transformer-based physics-informed learning to solve partial differential equations.

It is evaluated on problems including reaction, convection, wave equations, Burgers equation, and 2D Navier-Stokes equations.

## What is Safe Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for UAVs?

Safe Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for UAVs is a research framework for autonomous UAV behaviors with explicit safety constraints.

It combines multi-agent reinforcement learning with safety mechanisms such as Control Barrier Functions and Model Predictive Safety Control.

## Does SOAI work on AI safety?

Yes.

AI safety is part of the Technical Department's responsibilities. SOAI promotes responsible and ethical AI development by raising awareness of safety, alignment, and ethical considerations in technical work.

## Does SOAI do AI research?

Yes.

AI research is one of the responsibilities of the Technical Department. SOAI also develops research-oriented projects involving areas such as reinforcement learning, robotics, physics-aware machine learning, and safe autonomous systems.

## Does SOAI provide educational activities?

Yes.

SOAI provides educational opportunities through workshops, learning activities, educational content, and practical projects.

These opportunities can be accessible to members across the organization.
